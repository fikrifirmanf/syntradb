# Code of conduct

SyntraDB welcomes people with all kinds of backgrounds and skills. Treat others
with respect in every project space.

## Expected conduct

- Discuss technical ideas and evidence. Critique the work without attacking people.
- Give useful feedback. Accept corrections when the facts change.
- Respect different needs and ways of working.
- Help make the project usable for people with disabilities.
- Respect people's privacy and boundaries.
- Help keep discussions focused on the project.

## Unacceptable conduct

- Harassment, threats, bullying or hateful remarks.
- Attacks based on race, sex, gender, age, faith, disability or who someone loves.
- Unwanted sexual remarks, images or contact.
- Personal attacks, repeated unwanted contact or attempts to derail discussions.
- Sharing someone's private details or messages without their consent.
- Punishing someone for raising a concern or helping review a report.

This policy applies in project issues, pull requests, discussions and other
project spaces. It also applies when someone publicly represents SyntraDB.

## Reporting

Report concerns privately to the project maintainer at
[dev@fikrifirmanf.com](mailto:dev@fikrifirmanf.com). Include what happened,
where it happened, and any relevant links or evidence. Share only information
you are comfortable providing. Avoid posting incident details publicly.

The maintainer will review reports and keep details private when possible.
Some details may need to be shared to review a report, apply this policy or
protect someone from harm. We cannot promise full secrecy.

## Enforcement

The maintainer can remove posts, ask someone to change their conduct, issue
warnings, or block access for a set time or permanently. The response depends on
the harm caused and whether it has happened before. Serious abuse can lead to
an immediate ban.

People affected by a decision may ask for a review at the same email address.
Explain what facts or context the maintainer should consider.
